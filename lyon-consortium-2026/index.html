Art of Network Exhibition at the 4P-CAN Consortium Meeting in Lyon, France

March 23-24, 2026

  • ← Back to Home

As part of the 4P-CAN Consortium meeting in Lyon on March 23-24, 2026, the Art of Network exhibition was presented at the International Agency for Research on Cancer (IARC) headquarters.

The exhibition translated community data into visual storytelling, sharing real experiences from people living in Lerești. It showed, in a clear and accessible way, how citizen engagement, local collaboration, and data sharing can strengthen primary cancer prevention.
