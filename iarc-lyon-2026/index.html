The Lerești Art Gallery was presented in Lyon at the International Agency for Research on Cancer

March 24, 2026

  • ← Back to Home

We are pleased to share that the Living Lab art gallery was presented on March 24, 2026, at the headquarters of the International Agency for Research on Cancer (IARC) in Lyon.

This moment highlights the role of the Lerești Living Lab as a real-world innovation ecosystem, where community-driven insights, expressed also through artistic formats, contribute to advancing cancer prevention and health promotion at the European level.

The art gallery is conceived as a tool for citizen and interested stakeholders' engagement, an essential element for stimulating participatory research and strengthening collaboration between communities, researchers, and public health institutions.
